## Human character and animations

**Creator:** Quaternius. **License:** CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication).

- [Universal Base Characters](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html), free Standard edition, `Superhero_Male_FullBody`.

- Original license texts are bundled in `public/models/quaternius/LICENSE-BASE-CHARACTERS.txt` and `LICENSE-ANIMATIONS.txt`.

The GLB conversions were obtained from the public [Blood League asset mirror](https://github.com/Seyamalam/blood-league-kickoff/tree/main/public/assets/vendor/quaternius). Its [asset manifest](https://github.com/Seyamalam/blood-league-kickoff/blob/main/docs/ASSET_CREDITS.md) identifies the source Quaternius packs and CC0 license. Only these model / animation data and their license files were used; no game source or other artwork from that repository was copied.

The character GLB is unchanged. At runtime the game applies its own football kit colours, printed number, boots, socks and short-hair shading. Skin textures remain from the base character. `scripts/trim-animations.py` reduces the animation GLB to `Idle_Loop`, `Walk_Loop`, `Jog_Fwd_Loop` and `Sprint_Loop`, their bone rotations and pelvis motion, without rendering mannequin geometry. Runtime retargeting preserves character bone lengths. Shield, shuffle, turn, tackle and feint poses are implemented in this project.
